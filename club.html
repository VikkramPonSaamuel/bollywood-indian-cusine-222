BEFORE YOU ARRIVE

A little planning. A better night.

When does the Club open?

The Club opens every night from 8 PM to 2 AM, with the DJ starting at 8 PM. Any later closing is conditional and is not guaranteed.

Is there an entry or table charge?

There is no entry or table charge on non-event nights. Food and drinks are charged separately. Special events may have different terms.

What should we wear?

Please wear appropriate club attire. Outside drinks are not allowed.

Can guests bring children?

The Club is an adult-oriented nightlife venue. Contact management before planning a visit with children. Admission is not guaranteed and remains subject to applicable entry requirements and management approval.

Where is the entrance?

Enter Bollywood Indian Cuisine Seminyak and take the staircase by the entrance to the indoor restaurant. The Club is upstairs.

Is my online enquiry a confirmed booking?

No. Send your enquiry to our team and wait for confirmation of availability and any applicable terms. No payment or automatic reservation is taken by this preview.
