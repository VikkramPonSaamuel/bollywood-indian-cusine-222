OUR HOSPITALITY JOURNEY

Indian at heart. Across generations.

A family journey through hotels, food, travel and wellness — from Madras to Bali and Phuket.

19682026
  1. 1968

    Where the family story began

    Sri SSSA. Vivekanandam founded Vivekas Tourist Hotel Private Limited in Madras, beginning the family’s hospitality legacy.

  2. 1989

    A new chapter in Kanyakumari

    Sri SSSA. Vivekanandam expanded the business to Kanyakumari with a new hotel and accommodation hub.

  3. 1991

    Food and beverage become part of the welcome

    The company introduced full food and beverage services in its Madras and Kanyakumari hotels.

  4. 1993

    A welcome connected across India

    A PAN-India network of travel agents was established to coordinate accommodation and food logistics.

  5. 2009

    Balinese wellness comes to South India

    Grandson Gnana Ganesh Yogarajan and his wife Darsini Dwi brought Balinese wellness home, launching Taksuu Bali Spa in Madurai and Coimbatore.

  6. 2011

    The journey reaches Bali

    Gnana Ganesh Yogarajan and Darsini Dwi took the family legacy international with Tebing Villa, offering luxury accommodation in Ubud, Bali.

  7. 2013

    Bollywood Indian Cuisine begins

    Gnana Ganesh Yogarajan and his Balinese wife Darsini Dwi launched Bollywood Indian Cuisine, with Indian restaurants in Ubud, Seminyak and Nusa Penida.

  8. 2026

    From Bali to Phuket Old Town

    The family expanded Bollywood Indian Cuisine to Phuket Old Town, Thailand, carrying its hospitality and Indian dining into a new destination.
