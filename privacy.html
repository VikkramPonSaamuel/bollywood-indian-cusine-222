How enquiries work.

A clear handover to the branch — without an automatic booking confirmation.

Preparing an enquiry

The reservation form prepares a message in your browser. It does not send the message to a restaurant server, confirm a table or promise a response time. The form does not save your enquiry to browser storage.

Sharing your message

After reviewing your details, choosing “Open WhatsApp” passes the prepared message to WhatsApp. You still need to press Send there. A call-only contact requires a phone call. Your branch must confirm the booking directly.

External services

Links to WhatsApp, Google Maps, Google Drive, Google Forms, Instagram, Tripadvisor and other external services open those providers. Their own privacy practices apply. Web hosting and font providers may receive normal technical requests when a page loads.

Analytics

No third-party analytics service is enabled in this review copy. The developer has local event hooks available for a future, approved analytics setup. Enquiry names, dates and notes are not included in those events.

Questions

Contact the relevant branch directly with questions about your enquiry or the information you share.

Display preferences and Instagram

Your Dark or White view preference is saved in this browser. Selected Instagram Reels are loaded only when you choose Load Reel; this connects to Instagram, which may use its own cookies or require sign-in. Close a frame to unload it.
