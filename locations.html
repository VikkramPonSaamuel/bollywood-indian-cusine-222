Indian at heart. Across generations.

Our family’s hospitality roots go back to 1968 in Madras. The journey later reached Bali, with Tebing Villa in 2011 and Bollywood Indian Cuisine in 2013. In 2026, the restaurant family expanded to Phuket Old Town.
